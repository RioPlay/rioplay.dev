I'm RioPlay. I like taking things apart to see what else they can do, and this is where I write about it.

== Where this started

My first real personal computer was a Chromebook. It was supposed to be a browser with a keyboard attached, a locked-down thing that did a short list of approved tasks and nothing else. I spent a lot of my time growing up ignoring that list. Most of what I know now started as me trying to make that little machine do something it was never meant to, then figuring out why it broke when I did.

That habit never went away. It just moved on to bigger machines and harder problems.

== What this site is

This is a personal blog, not a single-topic one. I expect it to wander across whatever I am building, breaking, learning, or curious about: developer tooling, the projects I make, the occasional rabbit hole. The through-line is not a subject, it is the way I like to approach things.

The reason it exists at all is link:/series/aden/[*Aden*], a tool I have been building that turns a codebase into a queryable map for humans and AI agents. Aden is the first project I cared enough about to want to write it down and share it as I go, and once I started, a blog felt like the right home for the rest of my interests too.

A couple of corners worth knowing about:

* The link:/glossary/[Glossary] defines recurring terms once and links out to their sources, so posts can stay readable without re-explaining the basics.
* The link:/archives/[Archive] is the full index of everything published.

The site is built with Hugo and written in AsciiDoc, the same plain-text format Aden reads. I liked the idea of writing about the tool on the same substrate the tool is built on.
